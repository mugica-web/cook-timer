How to Play

1. One person gives the clues

That person holds the tablet or phone so nobody else can see it. Everybody else is guessing.

2. Describe the big word on top

Talk, and get your team to say that word out loud. When they say it — tap GOT IT and a new card appears.

3. But the red words are TABOO

You may not say any of the five red words. You also may not say the answer itself, or any piece of it — no "dogs" for DOG, no "sun" for SUNSHINE.

No spelling it, no rhyming it ("it rhymes with cat"), and no acting it out. Just words!

4. The other team watches

They peek over your shoulder at the card. If you slip and say a taboo word, they tap BUZZ — that card is gone and you get nothing for it. No points lost though!

5. Stuck? Skip it

Tap SKIP for a fresh card any time. Skips are free, but the clock keeps running.

6. When the timer hits zero

Your turn is over. You score one point for every card your team got. Then it's the next team's turn.
